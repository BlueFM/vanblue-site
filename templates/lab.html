Two inverted Gaussians, plus a shallow quadratic bowl that only keeps the trajectories on screen.

Optimisers

SGD:  p ← p − η∇f
SGDM:  v ← βv − η∇f,  p ← p + v
DIESGD:  s1 ← s1 + α(∇f − s1),  s2 ← s2 + α(s1 − s2),  p ← p − ηs2

MIE

s(0) = ∇f
proportional term
s(j) ← ξ s(j) + α s(j−1)
integral orders
λ(j) = softplus(λ̂(j))
so λ > 0
λ̃(j) = −α (g⊤s(j)) / ((‖g‖ + ε)(‖s(j)‖ + ε)) · σ(λ̂(j))
g = ∇f
m(j) ← μm(j) + (1 − μ)λ̃(j)
smoothed
λ̂(j) ← λ̂(j) − ηhm(j)
λ(0) > 0
p ← p − α[(∇f − ∇f−) + Σj λ(j)s(j)]
full step

“Sharpness” is the trace of the Hessian at the resting point, estimated by finite differences — a local curvature proxy, not a generalisation bound.

A toy, not an experiment: two wells, hand-tuned constants, no data and no training. The honest parts are the landscape, the update rules and the curvature readout.
